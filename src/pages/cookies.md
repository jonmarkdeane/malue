## Cookie Notice

**Last updated: 14 August 2026**

This notice explains how Malue Ltd uses cookies and similar technologies on this website. It should be read together with our Privacy Notice. 

### What cookies are 

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work, to make them work more efficiently, and to provide information to the website operator. 

### The cookies we use 

| Cookie / technology | Provider | Purpose | Duration |
|---------------------|----------|---------|----------|
| Strictly necessary cookies | Malue / website platform | Security, load balancing, and core website functions. These are required for the website to operate and do not need consent. | Session, or up to 12 months |
| Consent preference cookie | Malue / consent tool | Records the cookie choices you have made so that we do not ask you again on every visit. | Up to 12 months |
| _ga | Google Analytics | Distinguishes visitors so that we can measure how the website is used (pages visited, visit duration, approximate location at city level). Used only with your consent. | Up to 2 years |
| _ga_<container-id> | Google Analytics | Maintains the session state for Google Analytics measurement. Used only with your consent. | Up to 2 years |

 

Analytics information is collected in a way that does not directly identify you, and we do not combine it with information you provide through enquiries. IP addresses are truncated or processed in accordance with the provider's UK data protection commitments. 

### Your choices 

When you first visit the website you will be asked whether you accept optional cookies. Analytics cookies are not set until you choose to accept them. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time through the “Cookie settings” link on the website. 

You can also control cookies through your browser settings, including blocking or deleting them. Blocking strictly necessary cookies may affect how the website functions. 

### Changes and contact 

We may update this notice when the technologies we use change. If you have questions about our use of cookies, contact info@malue.co.uk.
